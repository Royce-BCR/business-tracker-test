# What's new in the Business Tracker

For Lynton, Gerry and Mike. This describes what has changed on the **test site** across the seven stages of the rebuild, page by page, and where to find each thing in the new menu. Nothing here is on the live site yet.

Nothing you have already typed has been changed, renamed or removed. Every page that existed before is still there; some have moved into a different menu group.

## The menu

The menu is now in seven groups. The same order and names show in the side menu, the narrow icon rail and the phone bottom bar. Light lines separate the sub-groups in the longer ones. You can still star favourites and collapse the menu.

| Group | Pages |
| --- | --- |
| **Command** | Today, Calendar and capacity · Dashboard, Reports |
| **CRM** | Contacts, Companies · Projects, Tasks |
| **Commercial** (Selling on the rail) | Lead Pipeline, Sales Pipeline, Quotes, Customer invoices |
| **Operations** | Project Pipeline · Suppliers, Orders and deliveries, Supplier invoices · Assets |
| **Finance** | Cash flow, Transactions, Bank Reconciliation · Profit and loss, Balance sheet, Budget and forecast, Ageing · Classify, Pricing Model |
| **Business** | Business tasks, Business costs, Business Plan |
| **Settings** | Settings |

Where things went: *Approvals* is now the **Review** tab on Quotes. *Invoicing* is **Customer invoices**. *Revenue Forecast* is **Budget and forecast**. *Purchase Orders* is **Orders and deliveries**. *Actions* and *Setup and Admin* are together on **Business tasks**. *Cash Flow Forecast* is **Cash flow**.

The **Monthly summary** has a link at the top of Today, at the top of the Dashboard, a button on the Reports page and a link on every other report.

## Page by page

### Today (Command)
A day view: arrows and a date picker. A time-critical block at the top (deliveries to sign off, invoices to raise, orders to place, prices to confirm), then job actions, business actions, projects today and phases due. Tick a delivery to sign it off, or press **All received** for one tap. Tick an invoice to mark it sent. Phases that are due can be ticked, then you confirm the hours. The list length is a setting (default ten, with See more).

### Calendar and capacity (Command)
Weekly hours wanted against hours available, with layers for confirmed projects, weighted sales and leads, deliveries and business tasks. Over-capacity weeks list what is in them. Click a day or week to open it on Today.

### Dashboard and Reports (Command)
The Dashboard keeps its cards. Cost of selling moved to Reports, with a link left behind. **Reports** is a library: business reports (monthly summary, ageing, profit and loss, cash flow, pipeline against break-even, capacity, cost of selling) and job reports for one project at a time. Every report shows the date range and a data-quality strip in the same place, and every figure opens the record behind it.

### Contacts, Companies, Projects, Tasks (CRM)
Contacts and companies have a record page with a customer lifecycle (lead, opportunity, customer, referrer, evangelist). **Projects** is the one list of every lead, sale and project. **Tasks** is the task queue: one task form for job or business tasks, repeats, and links to projects, customers, suppliers, quotes and orders.

### Record pages (project, supplier, contact, company)
Three columns: profile on the left, tabs in the middle, linked records on the right. Both side columns can be collapsed. A project has Overview, Activity, Plan, Materials and deliveries, and Money. If the middle column is too narrow for every tab, the rest go into a **More** menu; the tab you are on always stays visible. Changing a project date opens a reschedule review: Confirm schedule, Keep as tentative, or Cancel.

### Lead Pipeline, Sales Pipeline, Project Pipeline (Commercial and Operations)
Leads carry ballpark value, duration, preferred start, deadline and job type. Qualification questions (set in Settings) give a score badge and can stop a lead being promoted until a hard-gate question is answered well. **Closed lost** has an archive and a Reopen button on all three boards. The Project Pipeline shows six stages worked out from confirmed dates (Planning, Materials, On site, Sign-off, Invoiced, Completed); nothing stored is changed.

### Quotes and Customer invoices (Commercial)
Sending a quote creates a follow-up task in working days. **Chased** logs how you chased. The quote shows days since contact and next follow-up. Each material line can hold several supplier prices with lead time and valid-until, and a **Compare suppliers** card shows the whole basket per supplier. **Review** (formerly Approvals) is a tab on Quotes.

### Suppliers, Orders and deliveries, Supplier invoices, Assets (Operations)
One order per supplier from the accepted quote, and a second order for the same supplier when needed. Marking a delivery received opens a checklist (received, short, substituted, damaged, notes, photos). Confirm final prices now or later, with the invoice or receipt attached. Differences from the quote are shown as variances by cause. Any line can be marked as **hire**. Assets gain a usage charge and a buy-against-hire comparison.

### Finance
- **Cash flow**: a weekly review (cash now, lowest point in eight weeks, what is owed to you and by you, this week's items marked **Landed**, **Late** or **Moved**) and the earlier forecast.
- **Transactions**: each money-out line can be a job cost, business cost or asset purchase.
- **Profit and loss**: by month, with business costs beside, an Unclassified line, and a check against the bank.
- **Balance sheet**: a simple view, to be checked against Mike's accounts.
- **Ageing**: money owed to you and money you owe, by how late.
- **Classify**: accept suggested classes one at a time or all at once (it asks first).

### Business
**Business tasks**, **Business costs** (recurring costs that feed the cash flow forecast, with break-even sales) and the **Business Plan**. Business tasks and costs are visible to Lynton and Royce by default (Settings, CRM and tasks).

### Settings
New sections: Selling (job types, lifecycle names, qualification questions, follow-up gap), Planning and capacity (bank holidays, phase template, people and weekly hours), and Finance (set-aside method per pot, tool cost on jobs).

## Small things worth knowing
- Old **Mark paid** on a project warns you if the bank already shows that payment, and takes you to **Landed** so it is not counted twice. You can still mark it paid after confirming.
- On a phone, the buttons in dialogs stay at the bottom of the screen so a delivery can be signed off with one thumb.
- Every list page has the same layout: title and the one main button at the top right, a row of counts, filters, then the list or board.
